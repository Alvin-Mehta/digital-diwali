# Cracker Documentation: Golden Phuljhadi (Handheld Sparkler)

## 1. Overview
The Phuljhadi is the quintessential handheld celebratory sparkler. Coated along a wire rod, it burns gracefully downwards while spitting a continuous constellation of dazzling golden, amber, and violet sparks.

## 2. Animation Phases
1. **Idle:** Wire sparkler upright on stage.
2. **Ignite:** Match flame touches top tip.
3. **Fuse Burning:** Brief ignition sizzle (600ms).
4. **Sparkling Burn (Active & Peak):** The spark emitter starts at the top and tracks downwards as the chemical composition burns away. High-velocity branching sparks spray outwards in 360 degrees. Gentle waving adds lifelike organic motion.
5. **Decay:** Final embers dissipate, leaving the spent wire rod.

## 3. Audio Choreography
- Match strike: Friction scratch.
- Fuse: Initial sizzle.
- Sputtering Sparkle: High-frequency twin band-pass stochastic micro-clicks (4.5 kHz & 8 kHz) crackling continuously for 4.2 seconds.
