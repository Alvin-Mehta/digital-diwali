# Cracker Documentation: Royal Anar (Flower Pot)

## 1. Overview
The Anar is a ground-based conical terracotta pot filled with energetic metallic granules. Once ignited at its conical apex, it produces a majestic, upward-surging fountain of golden, silver, and crimson sparks reaching multiple meters high.

## 2. Animation Phases
1. **Idle:** Conical clay pot sits solidly in the center of the celebration stage.
2. **Ignite:** Matchstick descends to touch the top tip fuse.
3. **Fuse Burning:** Fuse sparkles at the apex for 800ms.
4. **Eruption (Active & Peak):** A high-density cone emitter sprays upwards at angles between 240° and 300° (upwards). Sparks accelerate with high initial velocity and arc over gracefully under gravity.
5. **Decay:** Fountain amplitude softens; embers drop to the floor and burn down.

## 3. Audio Choreography
- Match strike: Friction scratch.
- Fuse: Sulfur fizz.
- Fountain: Multi-band crackling hiss swelling steadily to a crescendo before fading into gentle crackles.
