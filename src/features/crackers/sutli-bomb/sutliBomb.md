# Cracker Documentation: Thunder Sutli Bomb

## 1. Overview
The Thunder Sutli Bomb is an iconic green twine-wrapped explosive. Famous for building heart-pounding suspense as the thick braided fuse fizzes down, followed by a split-second pause and a thunderous concussive boom that shakes the entire celebration courtyard.

## 2. Animation Phases
1. **Idle:** Solid green jute bomb sitting prominently on stage.
2. **Ignite:** Matchstick flame ignites top rope fuse.
3. **Fuse Burning:** Thick cord burns down vigorously (1000ms).
4. **Suspense Pause:** 150ms moment of breathless silence.
5. **Detonation (Peak):** Full-screen white flash bloom accompanied by rapid radial dispersion of paper fragments, jute fibers, smoke clouds, and fire sparks.
6. **Decay:** Residual smoke billows and slowly dissolves.

## 3. Audio Choreography
- Match strike: Friction scratch.
- Fuse: Deep, heavy sulfur fizz.
- Detonation: Hard-clipped transient transient crack + deep sub-bass 90 Hz -> 25 Hz boom with 800ms natural acoustic reverberation.
