# Cracker Documentation: Zameen Chakri (Ground Spinner)

## 1. Overview
The Zameen Chakri is a coiled pyrotechnic wheel placed flat on the ground. Once lighted on its outer rim, tangential thrust rapidly accelerates it into a whirling vortex of golden and emerald sparks, skittering playfully across the ground.

## 2. Animation Phases
1. **Idle:** Circular wheel resting flat on the celebration courtyard.
2. **Ignite:** Matchstick descends to the outer edge fuse.
3. **Fuse Burning:** Sizzling fuse burns for 800ms.
4. **Spin (Active & Peak):** The wheel rotates through hundreds of RPMs with tangential Canvas particle emissions spraying radially outwards in 360 degrees. Slight ground oscillation simulates physical floor friction.
5. **Decay:** Rotational speed decelerates smoothly; sparks thin out until coming to a peaceful halt.

## 3. Audio Choreography
- Match strike: Friction scratch.
- Fuse: Sulfur crackle.
- Whirring Spin: High-velocity FM sawtooth oscillator revving up from 120 Hz to 480 Hz and tapering back down to 80 Hz.
