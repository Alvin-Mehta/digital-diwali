# Color Palette & Festive Theming

## 1. Palette Philosophy
Diwali is the festival of triumph of light over darkness. The visual theme is rooted in deep midnight festival skies illuminated by warm diya oil lamps, radiant gold filigree, vibrant marigold orange, and festive royal amethyst.

---

## 2. Color Tokens

### 2.1 Jewel & Background Shades (The Night Sky)
- `--color-bg-deep`: `#0D071E` (Darkest imperial midnight purple, main canvas background)
- `--color-bg-surface`: `#1A0F36` (Deep royal violet, cards and navigation panels)
- `--color-bg-elevated`: `#281852` (Elevated card background, modal backdrop)
- `--color-bg-overlay`: `rgba(13, 7, 30, 0.85)` (Frosted backdrop blur overlay)

### 2.2 Festive Light & Diya Golds (The Illuminations)
- `--color-gold-400`: `#FCD34D` (Radiant Diya flame highlight)
- `--color-gold-500`: `#F59E0B` (Primary warm brass gold)
- `--color-gold-600`: `#D97706` (Deep antique gold border)
- `--color-diya-glow`: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)` (Radial ambient diya glow)

### 2.3 Marigold & Festive Accents
- `--color-festive-orange`: `#FF6B00` (Vibrant marigold orange)
- `--color-festive-red`: `#E11D48` (Sindoor crimson red, Sutli bomb accent)
- `--color-festive-pink`: `#EC4899` (Gulal pink sparkle)
- `--color-festive-teal`: `#14B8A6` (Peacock feather teal)
- `--color-festive-green`: `#10B981` (Fresh mango leaf green)

### 2.4 Neutral & Text Tokens
- `--color-text-primary`: `#FFFBEB` (Warm candlelight ivory, highest contrast)
- `--color-text-secondary`: `#E2D9F3` (Soft lavender silver, subtitles and metadata)
- `--color-text-muted`: `#9B8BB6` (Muted dusk lavender, placeholders and hints)
- `--color-border-subtle`: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.2)` (Golden gossamer border)
- `--color-border-active`: `rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.6)` (Selected cracker ring)

---

## 3. WCAG 2.2 AA Contrast Compliance
- Text Primary (`#FFFBEB`) on Background (`#0D071E`): **Contrast Ratio 15.8:1** (Passes AAA)
- Text Secondary (`#E2D9F3`) on Surface (`#1A0F36`): **Contrast Ratio 8.4:1** (Passes AAA)
- Gold Button Text (`#1A0F36`) on Gold Button (`#F59E0B`): **Contrast Ratio 6.5:1** (Passes AAA)
